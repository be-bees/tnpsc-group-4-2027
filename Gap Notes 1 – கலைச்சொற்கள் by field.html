Gap 1 · How TNPSC asks கலைச்சொல் + the 2025 questions (with Day 51)

Asked: 2025 Q28, Q29, Q30, Q31, Q32, Q33, Q40, Q41, Q80, Q81, Q82, Q83, Q93, Q94 · 2024 Q46, Q73 · 2016 Q25

The textbook boxes "கலைச்சொல் அறிவோம்" (Class 6–10) are already collected in Day 51 sheet – learn that first; nothing from it is repeated here. This file adds the terms that come from outside the textbooks.

1. Where the 2025 words came from

  • In 2025 the unit jumped from 1–2 questions to 14. Only 1 (Irrigation Technology) was from a textbook box.
  • 12 of the other 13 are word-for-word in the Tamil Virtual Academy (தமிழ் இணையக் கல்விக்கழகம்) கலைச்சொல் பேரகராதி – the subject-wise English → Tamil glossary on tamilvu.org (subjects: Administration, Agriculture, Banking, Biology, Chemistry, Economics, Engineering & Technology, Environmental Science, Geology, History, Home Science, Information Technology, Law, Medicine, Physics …). Source: tamilvu.org – கலைச்சொற்கள். All Tamil terms in Gaps 2–9 were checked in this glossary unless marked otherwise.
  • Whitehall is also in the old College Tamil Committee "கலைச் சொல்லகராதி – வரலாறுகள்" (White Hall – பிரிட்டிஷ் அரசாங்கப் பணிமனை) on ta.wikisource; Proposal – கருத்துரு and Call Book also in the TN Govt "தலைமைச் செயலகச் சிறப்புச் சொற்கள் துணை அகராதி".
  • So the pool is large. Strategy: (1) Day 51 fully; (2) the everyday GS / office / IT words in Gaps 2–9; (3) for an unknown word use the field clue in the question and eliminate options from other fields.
