# TNPSC Group 4 (CCSE-IV) 2027 – study notes and 73-day plan

Self-study notes for the **TNPSC Combined Civil Services Examination – IV (Group 4)**, exam on
**Sunday 10 January 2027**, built from the Tamil Nadu State Board textbooks (Class 6–12) and checked against
**1,200 past exam questions (2016–2025)**.

**▶ Start here: [https://be-bees.github.io/tnpsc-group-4-2027/](https://be-bees.github.io/tnpsc-group-4-2027/)** – the 73-Day Master Guide. Open it on a phone or PC and follow today's card.

## What is in it

- **29 notes files, about 446,503 words**, covering every syllabus unit:
  Tamil (Part A), General Studies (Part B) and Aptitude & Reasoning (Part C).
- **The exam pattern behind them:** every question of six past papers (2016, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2024, 2025)
  was traced to the textbook lesson it came from and to the notes section that answers it.
- **"★ Asked before" boxes** at the top of 109 notes sections list the exact points past papers asked from that section.
- **Gap Notes** cover everything past papers asked that is not in the textbooks, so all 1,200 past questions are
  answered somewhere in the notes: 600 in the day notes, 504 in Gap Notes 1–5, 96 one-liners in Gap Notes 6.
- **A 73-day plan** (29 Oct 2026 – 9 Jan 2027) with time slots, daily drills, weekly sectional tests,
  10 full mocks, score checkpoints and a revision system.

## How to study each day

1. Open today's card in the [Master Guide](https://be-bees.github.io/tnpsc-group-4-2027/).
2. In each slot (Tamil, Thirukkural, GS, Aptitude) read the **★ Asked before** box first, then the section.
3. Do the section's **Quick test** (answers are folded – tap "Show answers").
4. Drill 10–20 real past questions for the unit (the "PYQs" link on the card).
5. Evening: a 20-question quiz; write every wrong answer in a mistake log as one line.
6. Next morning, 5 minutes: re-read yesterday's ★ boxes and mistake-log lines.

| Phase | Days | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Learn | 1–49 (29 Oct – 16 Dec) | One notes section per slot per day; weekly test on Saturdays; full mocks from Day 25 |
| 2 · Fill the gaps | 50–56 (17–23 Dec) | Tamil unit sheets + Gap Notes, Geography, recent current affairs |
| 3 · Past papers + mocks | 57–63 (24–30 Dec) | Full past papers timed, mistake log, weakest unit |
| 4 · Final revision | 64–73 (31 Dec – 9 Jan) | High-Yield Sheet → ★ boxes → Gap Notes → Kural → formula sheet; light last three days |

## What past papers show (summary)

- **Asked in all six papers:** Thirukkural, HCF/LCM, word meanings (சொல் – பொருள்).
  In five of six: கலைச்சொல் (technical terms), simple/compound interest, வேர்ச்சொல்.
- **Asked in every recent paper (2022, 2024, 2025):** ஓரெழுத்து ஒருமொழி, எதிர்ச்சொல், பிரித்து எழுதுதல்,
  ஒலி மரபு, பழமொழி, number series, coding.
- **Most-tested GS lesson:** Class 10 Social Science, Civics Unit 1 (Indian Constitution) – 14 questions in 5 papers.
- **2025 shifted Part A** from literature (56 → 14 questions) to grammar (40) and technical terms (1–2 → 14).
  Its technical terms match the Tamil Virtual Academy subject glossaries; its reading passage and three aptitude
  questions were taken word for word from the textbooks.

## Sources and accuracy

- **Textbooks:** Tamil Nadu State Board (Samacheer Kalvi) books, Class 6–12, 2024–2025 editions – Tamil-medium
  books for Tamil, English-medium books for GS and Maths. Each day's notes give the book, unit and PDF pages.
- **Past papers:** TNPSC Group 4 papers 2016, 2018, 2019, 2022, 2024, 2025. 2025 answers follow TNPSC's
  official final answer key; other years were answered from the textbooks (uncertain answers are marked ⚠).
- **Thirukkural:** kural text and மு. வரதராசனார் உரை checked against a full 1,330-kural source.
- **Outside facts** (current affairs, schemes, literature and technical terms not in the textbooks) come from
  official and reliable sources linked in the notes, or are marked "(extra)".
- Where a textbook is wrong or outdated, the notes give the textbook's version and the correct fact side by side.

## Known limits

- **Current affairs** cover January – October 2026; November – December 2026 will be added before the exam.
- **Class 10 Science, printed page 305:** the right-hand column is blank in the source PDF; read it in the printed book.
- Maps and diagrams are described in words, not reproduced.
