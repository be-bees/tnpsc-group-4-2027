  • Released Feb 2026 with the interim budget. TN's 2nd Economic Survey (the first came in March 2025). TNPSC Thervupettagam
  • GSDP 2024–25 (current prices) ₹31.19 lakh cr; nominal growth 16%; real growth 11.19% – highest among major states. TNPSC Thervupettagam
  • Per-capita income 2024–25 ₹3.62 lakh = 1.77 × national average (₹2.05 lakh); TN 3rd among states. TNPSC Thervupettagam

  • 2025–26 estimates (MoSPI/budget): real growth ≈11% (MoSPI-based reports 10.83%); nominal GSDP ₹35.29 lakh cr; per-capita GSDP ₹4,55,650 (current, +13%) vs India ₹2,43,803; per-capita income ~₹4.08 lakh, 2nd after Karnataka. Target: $1 trillion economy by 2030. PRS · South First
