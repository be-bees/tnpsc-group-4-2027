1 · How to study every day

  1. Open today's card below. Each slot (Tamil, Thirukkural, GS, Aptitude) links straight to its notes section. Study it in the slot's time.
  2. Read the ★ "Asked before" box first at the top of each section — those exact points came in past papers. Then read the section once, slowly.
  3. Do the section's Quick test (end of the section) without looking. Tick the answers.
  4. Drill past questions — the "PYQs" link under the slot opens that unit's real past questions (newest first). Do 10–20.
  5. 10:25 pm: daily quiz (20 Qs) → write every wrong or guessed question in the Mistake Log tab of your tracker (one line: the correct fact).
  6. Next morning, 5 minutes: re-read yesterday's ★ boxes and yesterday's Mistake Log lines.

Do not make long notes — the notes are already written. Write only the lines you keep getting wrong (Mistake Log) and the grammar rules you mix up (rules notebook).
