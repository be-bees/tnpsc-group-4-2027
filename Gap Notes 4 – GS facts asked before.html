11. National flag – Flag Code of India 2002

  • No other flag may be placed higher than or to the right of (i.e. above or on the honoured right side of) the National Flag – "to its left" is the wrong rule PYQ 2018.
  • Hoist briskly, lower slowly and ceremonially; stand at attention while it is hoisted PYQ 2018.
  • Older rule "sunrise to sunset"; since the July 2022 amendment a flag displayed in the open or on a citizen's house may fly day and night, and machine-made/polyester flags are allowed (extra).

6. Thirukkural in the GS paper

  • Thirukkural names no king, dynasty, place or religion – it is a universal work (உலகப் பொதுமறை). It speaks of kingship in general (இறைமாட்சி, செங்கோன்மை), not of the Moovendhar. So "Valluvar captured the pride and administration of the three great kings" is incorrect (2025 key: statement incorrect, reason correct) PYQ 2025.
  • வள்ளுவரும் குறளும் – கி.ஆ.பெ. விசுவநாதம் PYQ 2025.
  • First standard portrait of Thiruvalluvar – drawn by K.R. Venugopala Sharma (the officially accepted image) PYQ 2025.
  • "வள்ளுவர் செய்த திருக்குறளை … உணர்ந்தோர் உள்ளுவரோ மனுவாதி …" (those who know the Kural will not think of Manu's code) – P. Sundaram Pillai, in the invocation (Tamil Thai Vazhthu part) of Manonmaniyam PYQ 2024.

2. RNA

  • mRNA – carries the code for protein; tRNA – brings amino acids; rRNA – part of ribosomes (extra).
  • Non-coding RNAs do not code for protein; they regulate gene expression (miRNA, siRNA – gene silencing) and take part in replication/translation (tRNA, rRNA). 2025 key accepted "replication" or "gene regulation" PYQ 2025.

4. Human body – teeth, blood vessels, testes

  • Human teeth (extra): milk (deciduous) set = 20, formula per half-jaw I2 C1 PM0 M2 (2102); permanent set = 32 (2123). The milk "molars" are replaced by premolars; the true molars appear only in the permanent set – so the paper's answer for "teeth absent in milk dentition in mammals" was molars PYQ 2024 (books that write 2102 call the same point "no premolars" – read the options carefully).
  • Blood samples are taken from veins – blood in veins is under low pressure and veins lie near the surface (A and R true, R explains A) PYQ 2019.
  • Sertoli cells produce Androgen Binding Protein (ABP) (and inhibin – extra), besides nourishing sperm PYQ 2019.
  • Extra-embryonic membranes: amnion – amniotic fluid, a fluid medium that protects the embryo; chorion + allantois → placenta; yolk sac – nourishment (not the fluid medium) PYQ 2018.

7. Types of sexual fusion (lower organisms)

  • Hologamy – whole mature individuals behave as gametes and fuse PYQ 2019.
  • (extra) Merogamy – small gametes formed by division of the parent fuse; isogamy – similar gametes; anisogamy – gametes differ in size; oogamy – large non-motile egg + small motile sperm; paedogamy – young individuals/gametes of the same parent fuse; autogamy – gametes from the same cell fuse.

5. Earth and disasters

  • Rotation (on its axis) → day and night; revolution round the Sun + the 23½° tilt of the axis → seasons. So "rotation causes seasons" is false (A true, R false) PYQ 2018.
  • 0° latitude, 0° longitude lies in the Gulf of Guinea, Atlantic Ocean, south-west of Africa PYQ 2019.
  • Major floods: China – Hwang Ho ("China's sorrow"); Pennsylvania – Johnstown flood (1889, dam failure); Los Angeles – St. Francis Dam collapse (1928); India – Assam (Brahmaputra) PYQ 2018. Damodar was the "Sorrow of Bengal" (extra).
